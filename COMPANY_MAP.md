# Japan Company Map

## Status

Research database framework — Version 0.1

This document defines the framework for identifying Japanese companies, startups, research organizations, studios, and technology teams that may be relevant to the Personal AI Entertainment System project.

This is a research and collaboration database.

Inclusion in this document does not imply endorsement, partnership, or existing communication.

---

# 1. Research Objective

The objective is to identify organizations in Japan that possess capabilities relevant to the development of Personal AI Entertainment Systems.

The research focuses on the convergence of:

AI
+
Games
+
Characters
+
Animation
+
Music
+
Interactive Entertainment
+
Virtual Worlds
+
Consumer Electronics
+
Hardware
+
Edge AI

The objective is not to find one company capable of doing everything.

The objective is to identify complementary capabilities across the ecosystem.

# 8. Large Company Research

Large Japanese companies should be investigated as ecosystem anchors.

Potential categories include:

- Game platforms
- Consumer electronics
- Entertainment conglomerates
- Animation
- Character/IP
- Music
- Hardware
- AI

Large organizations should generally not be the first cold-contact targets.

They should initially be treated as:

- Industry benchmarks
- Technology references
- Ecosystem anchors
- Future strategic targets

# 9. Startup Research

Startups may be more suitable for early collaboration.

Priority should be given to organizations that demonstrate:

- Small or focused teams
- Emerging AI products
- Experimental entertainment
- Open innovation
- International collaboration

- APIs or SDKs

The objective is to find organizations capable of moving quickly.

# 14. Open Innovation

Open innovation should be treated as an important collaboration channel.

Relevant opportunities may include:

- Corporate innovation programs
- Startup programs
- University partnerships
- Accelerators
- Industry events
- Government-supported programs
- International business-matching programs

JETRO J-Bridge is one example of an international open-innovation platform connecting Japanese organizations with overseas startups and companies.

This project will investigate whether J-Bridge and similar programs can provide appropriate collaboration pathways.

# 16. Contact Strategy

Initial contact should be concise and evidence-based.

A potential contact package should eventually contain:

1. Short introduction

2. Project hypothesis

3. Technology map

4. Prototype or demonstration

5. GitHub repository

6. Specific collaboration proposal

7. Contact information

The objective is not to ask:

"Can you help me?"

The objective is to ask:

"Would you be interested in exploring this specific technical or product hypothesis together?"

---

# 17. Evidence Standard

Company information should be based primarily on:

- Official company websites
- Official product pages
- Official technical documentation
- Official corporate announcements
- Official research publications
- Government or industry databases
- Reputable industry publications

Social media should be treated as secondary evidence.

Unverified claims should be marked as unverified.

---

# 18. Data Quality

Every company record should include:

- Date researched
- Source
- Evidence
- Researcher notes

Information should be periodically reviewed because companies, products, leadership, and strategies change.
